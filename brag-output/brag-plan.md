# /brag plan — OpenNeko

**What it is:** A self-hosted agent that watches your business data, flags what's worth a look, and drafts the next action for you to approve.
**For:** Operators and CXOs at businesses (commerce, regulated ops) who need to hear about the risk before the customer or the P&L does.
**Different:** Actions are drafted, not auto-fired. Self-hosted: the memory (findings, rules, decisions) stays on your Postgres; the model is swappable.
**Best claim:** "The intelligence is rented; the findings, rules, and decisions are yours."
**Visual hook:** 2:14am on a black screen: something broke while you slept. OpenNeko was awake.
**Real UI:** The Command Deck briefing (apps/web/briefing-command-deck-prototype.html) — dark command strip, the "Act now" Apple Pay decision card, evidence chips, "Review the fix".
**Tone:** default leaning polished — calm, confident, a little dry (the joke: it broke at 2:14am and you slept fine).
**Share line:** It broke at 2:14am. OpenNeko found it, traced it, and drafted the fix. You approved it over coffee.
